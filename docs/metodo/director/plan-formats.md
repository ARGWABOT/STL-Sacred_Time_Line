# The formats of `plan.json`

*A procedure of the STL Studio director. The model reads this English version; its Spanish version, `plan-formats.es.md`, has the same parts and is the one people read and approve. A change is written in both. The inbox (`tools/director/bandeja.ts`) says when to read it: in every review, every urgency and every clarification, before writing in the plan.*

## The patch, the mission and its steps

- **It is written with targeted changes, without reading it whole** (accepted proposal pr_encargo_a_medida, point 1): what changes goes in a patch, `director/cambios.json` in the project's memory, and `node tools/director/bandeja.ts [--proyecto <id>] --aplicar` merges it into the plan and saves it only if it adds no errors (`validarPlan`). By key (`respuestas`, `revisiones`, `evaluacion`, `ejecutadas`, `reservaCorta`), by id (`misiones`, `propuestas`, `pedidos`, `fases`, `frentes`; `documentos`, by their path), `cambiar` (some fields by id, and an inner list by position: `{ "0": "…", "+": […] }`), `quitar` (by id) and `orden` (the order of a list, without taking anything out: `{ "misiones": ["t_b", "t_a"] }` puts those in that order, in the places they had; to reorder, never take out and add back, because in between what the person already delivered or started is left without its mission and the Studio refuses their decisions); everything else, whole; with null, it goes away (`src/director/parche.ts`). The patch is not kept in the memory: it is overwritten for the next one.
- Mission: `{ "id", "para": "<id de persona>", "fase", "frente", "titulo", "porQue", "pasos": [texto | { "texto", "ir": {…, "etiqueta"}, "hecho"?, "detalle"?, "opciones"?, "varias"?, "hazlo"?, "soloTu"?, "terminal"?, "copiar"? }], "entrega", "documentos"?, "criterios"?, "xp", "vigente"?, "alCierre"?, "despuesDe"?: [ids of missions, proposals or requests], "origen"?: [ids of the notes, proposals, requests or missions it comes from] }`. The experience (`xp`) goes from 10 to 50 according to the effort; 100 XP is a level. A mission born from a note or from something approved says so in `origen`, and each proposal that is executed names in its `ejecutadas` note the commit that did it: this way an idea can be followed end to end, and `bandeja.ts --listo` checks that each thread exists (`origenesQueNoExisten`, `ejecutadasSinCommit`).
- **A step that asks to choose** has `opciones` (2 to 6 short sentences, without `hecho`): the Studio shows them as buttons in the step, a click leaves the choice in the Inbox (`sobre: { "tipo": "mision", "id", "seccion": "Paso N", "eleccion" }`, signed) and marks the step. In the inbox it arrives as «Eligió …»: answer it and take it to the plan like any note. For another answer, there is the step's ✉. If the person already answered with the step's ✉, the Studio also offers «Ya te lo dije en mi comentario», which marks the step without choosing an option; in the inbox it arrives with its comment: answer it with what it says. An option that promises to tell more (with «✉», «te cuento» or «te copio») opens the step's comment when chosen: the choice and what is told arrive together in one note. **If the options are not exclusive** (what to add to something, which of several ideas; the owner's voice note nmvoi1e0etit, 25 October 2026), also give it `varias: true`: each option is checked and unchecked, and «Listo» delivers them together in a single note, with their `eleccion` in the step's order and joined with « · »; an option that starts with «Ninguna» or «Nada» does not go with the others. It arrives as «Eligió A · B»: answer it option by option. **If one has to wait before answering** (what something published takes to show), give it `esperar` (seconds, from 5 to 900): the Studio shows the countdown, large, and does not show the step's button or its options until it is over (without options, the button is enough); the text does not repeat the wait.
- **A terminal command**: if the step asks to run something in the person's terminal, the command goes in `terminal` (a single line), not in the text. The Studio shows it with «Abrir la terminal con esta orden» (it opens a new window, in the project's folder, with the command running; on Windows, Git Bash) and «Copiar»: nobody has to look for the terminal or where to paste it. The text says what the command does and what is seen at the end. It never goes with `hazlo`.
- **A text to paste somewhere else** (a form outside the Studio, a message to someone): it goes whole in `copiar`, and the Studio shows it in the step with «Copiar el texto», for as long as it is needed. Never only in what a command prints, which is lost when its window closes (the owner's voice note nmvrikd6ptit, 28 October 2026).
- **Checking a terminal step**: give it `comprobar: { "orden", "espera"? | "vacia"? }` whenever its result can be read: a read-only command (`git log`, `git status`, `git rev-parse`, `git show`, `git ls-files`, `git branch --show-current`, `git config --get`… or `existe <ruta>` / `no existe <ruta>` inside the project; no pipes) and what its output must contain (`espera`) or that it comes out empty (`vacia`). When the step is marked, the Studio runs it in the project's folder, without a model: if it holds, it says «Hecho: lo comprobé»; if not, the step is not marked and it says what it expected and what it found. A checked step already brings its proof: the inbox does not flag it with «Compruébalo». Next to the command, the Studio puts «Ya lo hice», which does the same as «Listo, ya lo hice» in the other steps (with its check, if it has one). **A step that does not work out traps nobody** (the owner's note of 22 October 2026: the command failed, the check did not let the step be marked and they could not go on with other missions): next to «Ya lo hice» there is «No me salió», and the notice of a failing check brings «No me sale: sigo con otra». Both deliver the mission as it is, with what happened in its note, and the next one stays in view. The delivery carries the step that did not work out (`Entrega.sinTerminar`), and the inbox shows it as «entregada sin terminar», with the unmarked steps (`pasosSinMarcar`): it is not approved for what was marked; the director does what is missing, if it can and it is approved, or returns it with the step corrected. A delivery like that does not seal the document its step asked to read and that was left unmarked. **A step outside the Studio that nothing checks by itself** (without `hecho` or `comprobar`) also brings «Ya lo hice: compruébalo tú» (accepted proposal pr_verificar_paso): it marks the step and launches the director only to check it, like «No lo entiendo» (`bandeja.ts --comprobar`), while the person goes on with their next mission; if it was not met, that step comes back as their first mission, with what was found. That is why such a step says in its `detalle` what to do if what is seen is something else (a page still showing the old version: Ctrl + F5 or wait a few minutes).
- **«Hazlo tú»**: a step with `hazlo` (without `opciones` or `hecho`) brings that button; a dialog says what you will do and, when confirmed, the assignment stays in the Inbox (`sobre: { "tipo": "mision", "id", "seccion": "Paso N", "hazlo": true }`, signed) and the step is marked. **It is not executed instantly**: in the inbox it arrives as «Hazlo tú»; do it in that review, with the project's rules, and answer it with the result and its `destino` (the Studio shows your answer in the step). If in the end it cannot be done, say so and give the step back. When the last step is marked, the mission is delivered; the review is not requested by itself nor offered in the celebration, even if it was the last mission: running out of missions is no reason to request it, because before that the person may want to read, comment or correct (the owner's voice note of 22 October 2026). They request it whenever they want, with «Pedir revisión», in the header.
- **The steps done inside the Studio are marked by themselves**: give them `hecho`, what proves it was done, and the Studio marks them as soon as it sees it (without a model), says «Hecho: lo vi en el Studio» and, if it was the last one, delivers the mission. `{ "que": "meta_fijada" | "meta_reabierta" }` (with `desde` ISO, so as not to count an earlier goal), `{ "que": "leido", "id": "docs/…", "seccion"? }`, `{ "que": "propuesta", "id" }`, `{ "que": "puerta", "id": "<fase>" }` or `{ "que": "nota", "tipo"?, "id"?, "seccion"?, "desde"? }` (a note in the Inbox). What happens outside the Studio (running a command, trying something) goes without `hecho`: it is marked by hand.
- **«No lo entiendo, explícamelo mejor»**: every step brings that button. A click leaves a note on the step (`sobre: { "tipo": "mision", "id", "seccion": "Paso N", "aclarar": true }`) and the Studio launches the director only for that, without the rest of the review (`bandeja.ts --aclarar`): it rewrites **only that step**, without changing its number (the marked ones go by number), with more context in `texto` and, if they are several actions, `detalle` (the list of smaller actions, which the Studio shows inside the step); it answers the note with its `destino` in the mission and closes with `--aclarar --listo`. If it is asked again, go one level deeper: it is the sign that the step was badly written.

## The proposals, the requests and the rest

- Proposal: `{ "id", "area", "titulo", "problema", "propuesta", "conecta", "fase", "reemplaza"?: [ids] }`.
- **What is replaced, out** (the owner's decision of 23 October 2026, «El paquete de cada revisión», part 10): a request or a proposal that replaces another says so in `reemplaza` (the ids of the requests or proposals it leaves without effect), instead of telling it only in its text. The code takes it out of what is pending (the director's queue, what waits for the closing, what a mission waits for, which then waits for what replaced it), the Studio shows it as «Reemplazado» in «La ruta» and the closing takes it to the archive, where it is searched by its id (`reemplazos`). `validarPlan` does not let through an id that does not exist, one that replaces itself, two that replace the same one or two that replace each other. The same goes for the decisions in the documents: the inbox opens with **«Lo vigente»**, those still in force, one line each (`decisionesVigentes`); a new note on the same decision replaces the previous one by itself, and the answer to a decision that leaves another one of another part without effect says so in `reemplaza` (the ids of the notes it replaces), which takes it out of there (`bandeja.ts --listo` does not close if it names something that is not another decision).
- **A note that brings several things is answered in parts** (request pd_partes_separadas, part 2 of the accepted proposal «Cada nota con su tipo»): if it brings, for example, an adjustment and an idea, its answer carries `partes`, two or more, each one `{ "tipo", "texto", "tratamiento", "destino"? }`. The `tipo` is one of `idea` (an idea for later), `ajuste` (changing something that already exists), `falla` (something fails), `pregunta` or `respuesta` (a reply: it clarifies something the director said), as you read it: if it does not match the one the person chose, you correct it and say so in that part's `texto`. Each part has its treatment and, if it is `integrada`, `aplazada` or `decision`, its `destino`, like the whole answer. The answer's `texto`, `tratamiento` and `destino` are still those of the main thing. The Studio shows each part by itself, under the answer, with its type, its treatment and its «Quedó en»; `validarPlan` does not let through a single part (with only one thing there are no parts), an unknown type or treatment, an empty part or a destination that does not exist, and `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close with a part that takes something to the plan without saying where it went (`respuestasSinDestino`).
- **The type of each note** (request pd_tipo_obligatorio): the person chooses it with one tap when leaving a note (`idea`, `ajuste`, `falla`, `pregunta`, `respuesta`), except where the place already says it (a question, a decision, a chosen option, «Hazlo tú», «No lo entiendo», «Ya lo hice: compruébalo tú»); the inbox shows it with each note. If they chose «No sé: elígelo tú», the answer carries `tipo`, as you read it, and `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close without it (`respuestasSinTipo`); a note from before without one, still unanswered, also gets its `tipo` (the inbox asks for it). Notes answered before stay «Sin tipo», with their own filter in the Inbox.
- What was done ahead: `{ "fase", "frente", "texto" }` (work already done that counts for a future phase).
- The next piece of your queue (request pd_paquete_listo): `"piezaSiguiente": { "cuando", "id": "<of your queue>", "archivos": [{ "ruta", "parte"? }], "comprueba" }`, written before closing while your queue has something. The Studio, without a model, launches the next review with the parts of those files (a function or a section; the whole file without `parte`) and the procedures the inbox names already inside the assignment, so it starts working instead of reading; with consecutive reviews, where the next one starts by itself, `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close without it (`piezaPorCerrar`).
- Request: `{ "id": "pd_…", "fase", "texto", "notas"?: [ids], "hecho"?: { "cuando", "nota" }, "reemplaza"?: [ids] }` (what was asked in a note, to be done until it has `hecho`). What goes after the goal has `"fase": "despues"`: it shows in «La ruta», at the end of the last phase, in «Después del lanzamiento», with its state, and it holds no gate (the owner's decision of 22 October 2026); the `destino` that leads to it names that last phase.
- A closing mission (the final test, the upload, what measures the whole gate) has `"alCierre": true`: like the one that crosses the gate, the Studio holds it while an accepted proposal or a request of its phase or earlier is still undone. A request that is done in the closing itself (the installation package, what has to be taken out before building it) also has `"alCierre": true`: it does not hold those missions, because they are the ones that bring it, and the one that needs it waits for it with `despuesDe`.
- A mission that needs something done first has `"despuesDe"`: the ids of the missions, proposals or requests that precede it. The Studio holds it while any of them is still undone (a mission, until it is delivered; a proposal, until it is executed or discarded; a request, until its `hecho`), shows it in «Lo que sigue» with what it waits for and opens it by itself when it is ready; `validarPlan` does not let through an id that does not exist or a mission before another one of the same person that it needs. This is how the mission to see what the director will do is scheduled, at its point, without bringing it forward.
- Lesson: `{ "titulo", "quien", "que", "fuente", "paraElProyecto" }`, always with a real source.
- The ids of the people are in the `proyecto.json` of its memory › `equipo` (the owner is always `titular`). Each project has its team.
- `validarPlan` (in `src/director/director.ts`) says everything the plan must meet.
