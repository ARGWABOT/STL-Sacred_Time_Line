# The review cycle

*A procedure of the STL Studio director. The model reads this English version; its Spanish version, `review-cycle.es.md`, has the same parts and is the one people read and approve. A change is written in both. The inbox (`tools/director/bandeja.ts`) says when to read it: in every review.*

## The steps (en marcha)

With what the inbox lists:
1. **Deliveries**: in `revisiones`, per mission: `{ "cuando": "<ISO now>", "veredicto": "aprobada" | "rehacer", "comentario": "…" }`. Concrete: what went well, what is missing. **What is marked is not enough: check it.** A step that happens outside the Studio (running a command, trying something, setting up an account) is marked by hand and the Studio cannot see whether it was done: the inbox flags it with «Compruébalo». Before approving, look for the evidence (the repository, the tool, what was left) and say in the comment what you checked; if there is none, do not take it as done: give the step back and say what you did not find. The same in every open mission with steps marked by hand. The person can also ask for it at the moment, with «Ya lo hice: compruébalo tú»: your answer, under the step, is the evidence; the route does not move forward on a step done outside without that check or yours.
2. **Notes**: in `respuestas`, per note id: `{ "cuando": "<ISO>", "texto": "…", "tratamiento": "…" }`. Every note has an answer, with your recommendation, and **its treatment** (the STL method, step 7): `integrada` (it was incorporated into the project), `rechazada` (it is not incorporated, and the answer says why), `aplazada` (it is kept for a later phase: say which, and write it there, in its planned missions or in a proposal of that phase; what lives only in an answer goes to the archive and nothing shows it again; that is why the inbox lists, in the current phase, each answer «aplazada» for it or for an earlier one that has neither its request nor its proposal, `aplazadasSinPedido`, until it has one), `decision` (there is an ambiguity or a conflict you do not resolve: raise it as a decision in the Studio) or `informada` (it only informed or asked). **It is chosen, not built**: the inbox gives with each note the treatments that fit it (`tratamientosDeNota`: a «Hazlo tú» or a decision in a document is not just informed; what the person chose or a question is not rejected) and its destinations at hand (`destinosDeNota`), and `--listo` does not close with a treatment that does not fit (`tratamientosQueNoCaben`). **The double check:** if it is `integrada`, `aplazada` or `decision`, or if it answers a decision in a document (unless you reject it), the answer says where it went in the plan with its `destino`, like a step's button (`{ "vista": "misiones", "ancla": "mision-<id>", "etiqueta": "…" }`, a proposal, a phase, or `{ "doc", "seccion" }` of a document on the list): the Studio shows it as «Quedó en: …», `validarPlan` checks that the place exists and `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close the review while one is missing. **What is asked stays as a request** (a request kept only in the text of an answer or in a sentence of the planned missions has no state and is lost from view): each note that asks for work and is not done in that same review, nor is already a proposal or a mission, leaves its request in `plan.pedidos`, with its phase, when it is answered, and the answer's `destino` leads to it (`{ "vista": "ruta", "fase": "<its phase>", "ancla": "pedido-<id>" }`, never to the whole phase, where it cannot be found; `--listo` does not close with an integrated or postponed answer whose button leads to «La ruta» without its anchor: `pedido-<id>`, `propuesta-<id>` or, if the phase plan changed, `r-plan`); when it is done, it gets `hecho` (when and how: the commit). The Studio shows them in «La ruta» with their state, the inbox lists them and the mission that closes the phase waits for them, as for an accepted proposal. Read the notes **all together** before answering: two ideas in different notes can be a single one. **And if a note changes what an earlier one said** (a decision, an option chosen in a step, any note), its answer says so in `reemplaza`, with the ids of the earlier notes: you mark it, the person does nothing more, and the old one leaves «What is in force» and shows as «Reemplazado» (plan-formats.md › «What is replaced, out»). **The urgent ones** (`urgente`: a checkbox, without a separate reason; read the note to judge how urgent it is) are a request for priority, not an order: **you still decide the route**. Normally they arrive already attended (CLAUDE.md › «Urgent goes alone»). If it truly goes first and it is approved or asked for in the Studio, do it now and leave its result as that rule says; if not, explain which mission or criterion must go first and why. Those that reach the review (because the Studio could not launch you) are put first by the inbox: treat them the same. Always say, in one sentence, whether it was truly urgent.
3. **Criteria**: in `evaluacion`, those already met with what was delivered: `{ "fecha", "estado": "cumplido" | "no_aplica", "nota": "why" }`.
4. **What comes next gathers everything said**: before closing, take the feedback to every document nobody has reviewed yet («Documents to review» in the inbox), integrated where it belongs and not as an appendix. Never ask to review a document that does not include what was already said. **And what was already done is not read again** (CLAUDE.md › «Critical rule: never ask again for what is already done»): for each document or step to be read, check whether what it explains, proposes or asks to decide was already done, decided or approved some other way (a note, a «Hazlo tú», several iterations, what was achieved); if so, take it out of the mission and say so in the answer. The inbox watches it: if since the last review there was work (commits in the repository, «Hazlo tú», notes on a mission), it lists each open mission and `--listo` does not close until the director, after checking it, gives it `vigente` (the ISO date of now); also the ones it writes in that review. The same with **everything pending in the plan**, not only the missions: an unanswered proposal whose work was already done or is already a mission leaves the plan, an accepted one that was already executed goes in `ejecutadas`, and the planned missions of each phase (`fases[].misiones`) are updated with what was done; if something changes the route, the following missions change with it. **And the code requires it**: if since the last review there was work (commits in the repository, also those of that same review, or «Hazlo tú»), `--listo` does not close until the director, after crossing with what was done each accepted proposal not executed, each request not done and the planned missions of each phase, writes down what it checked and what changed in `plan.alDia` (`{ "cuando", "nota" }`; `pendientePorComprobar`). The pending items that a commit since the last review names by their id or truly resembles are listed by the inbox in «It may already be done», with that evidence, and `--listo` does not close until each one is done or its id is in that note (`puedeQueYaEsteHecho`, `pistasSinAtender`). That is why **each person has only the next document to read**: the others are written when their turn comes, with whatever has happened by then (the inbox warns if there is more than one). What comes later lives in the plan (the phases, their missions and the proposals), not in documents written in advance.
5. **Decide again each person's next mission** with what was learned: if the one coming is no longer the right one, change it. **Trying the product on other computers goes only at the closing**: installing it on another computer, or giving it to someone from outside to try, is asked for only after the person has declared the work of the phase complete, as an `alCierre` mission that waits for that declaration with `despuesDe`; never before, not even to fill the reserve (fixed case c_prueba_antes_de_tiempo). **What is to be seen goes by topic, and it is never the whole batch**: work your queue in runs of several pieces per review, and what the person has to look at from that run goes in **one mission per topic** (a view, a function: what comes from the same request, proposal or note, named in its `origen`), **with one step per piece of that topic**, after the last one; never one viewing mission per piece of the same topic. Each one is delivered on its own, and the person comments on a topic without opening the others. What they have at hand is never only that (CLAUDE.md › «Always, without exception, the person has something to do or review»). `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close with two new viewing missions for the same person that share their `origen`, or one without it (`misionesDeVerSueltas`), nor when everything a person has at hand only asks to see a change (`soloVeUnCambio`, in `reservaSinRazon` and `loteSoloDeVer`). **And it is always ten** while your queue has anything (CLAUDE.md › «But never filler»): before writing them, go through your whole queue, not only its top, and ask what each item will need from the person. If ten real ones still do not come out, bring forward the decisions that the items further down will need, even those the person said they would take later: only of the current phase, each `porQue` saying that it is brought forward and why; never what belongs to another phase nor a test before its time. If a decision brought forward changes later, its mission says so. **Missions already queued stay valid**: if this review's changes do not touch or change them, they are not rewritten; check that, and write only those missing to reach ten, never the whole batch again (the inbox says how many).
6. `mensaje`: the director's comments on this review, in one to three bullets (one per topic, with its topic in bold), **without repeating what was achieved** (that goes in `resumen.avanzamos`; see «Dónde quedamos»). It is read in «¿Dónde quedamos?», after what was achieved. **Do not describe there the mission or what each one does today**: the Studio takes each person to their mission, and what they have to do goes in it (`personas` is no longer shown). If it is time, `boletin` (grades A to F per subject; grade the **focus**, not the quantity).
7. If the diagnosis of a front changed, update it with the date.
8. **Questions**: the inbox lists them separately («Questions without an answer»). They are notes with `sobre: { "tipo": "pregunta" }` that the owner writes down when they come up, so as not to interrupt their work, and they pile up like the comments until your review. The Studio shows them in «Preguntas», **apart from the comments**, and your answers are read **as a report**: one per review, together. Answer them in `respuestas` like any note, but written to be read on their own:
   - **The direct answer first** (yes, no, how much, which, when), then the why, in a few sentences. You can use Markdown (bold, lists, links): the report shows it formatted.
   - Complete: no need to open anything else to understand it. If it refers to something in the Studio or the repository, name it precisely.
   - `tratamiento`: almost always `informada`. If the answer reveals work to do, say so in the answer and take it to a proposal or a mission (`integrada`); if it asks the owner to decide, `decision`, with the decision in its place.
   - Read the questions and the notes together: a question can be answered with the same as another note.
   - **Conversations**: on an answer the owner can keep asking or commenting; that note carries in `sobre.id` the question it follows, and the Studio shows them together, folded. The inbox gives you what was already said in the conversation: answer in that context, without repeating what you already answered.

## «Dónde quedamos» (the summary of each project)

The first thing one asks when coming back to a project is «how are we doing, where did we leave off?». The Studio shows it to the owner every time they switch to that project, with a button straight to their current mission, and adds what is new since their last visit. The director writes it in `plan.resumen` at the end of **every** review of that project:
```json
"resumen": { "fecha": "<ISO>",
  "avanzamos": ["What was done, one line per advance, with its concrete result if there is one: newest first, at most 6"],
  "quedamos": "Where we left off: the state of the project (what was achieved, the phase, the goal), in one or two sentences. No pending items.",
  "alerta": "Only if there is a deadline or an active risk, in one line" }
```
Written for someone who comes from another project and remembers nothing: concrete and without jargon. The Studio shows it together with the `mensaje`, in two parts that **do not repeat each other**:
- **«Lo que avanzamos»** (`avanzamos`) is what was done: the only place where it is told. **One line per advance**, never two about the same thing: if a new advance continues or replaces an earlier one on the same topic, the old line goes. A line already written **is not rewritten** (each rewritten line shows up again to the owner as new): it is left as is or removed. The concrete result (what was measured, tested or delivered) goes inside its line, not separately (`resultados` is no longer written). **And it does not tell again what was already answered in a note**: what was done because of a note, a comment, a question or a failure is already told by its answer, which the person reads; what was achieved brings only what did not reach them that way (the work of your queue, what you did on your own). `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close with a new line that repeats an answer of that review (`avanzamosRepiteRespuestas`).
- **«Comentarios del director»** (`mensaje`) is what the director has to say that is not an advance: why it decided something, what it could not do, a warning, what is worth knowing. It never tells again what is already in `avanzamos` or what the previous comment already said (`bandeja.ts --listo` does not close if a sentence of the message repeats a line of what was achieved or a sentence of the saved message: rewritten or lengthened, the Studio would show it again as new). **It goes in bullets, one per topic**, each one starting with its topic in bold (`- **Sobre el candado:** …`): the Studio shows each bullet as a separate line, which is read and commented on by itself. If there is nothing to comment on, one sentence is enough. It serves to **recover the context** (what happened and where the project is), **not for pending items** (CLAUDE.md › «What is pending lives only in the mission»). The `alerta` is also seen in the list of projects: use it sparingly (a date, a risk in production), never to push work.
- **The answers to their notes** go apart, each in the tab of its type (questions, adjustments, failures, ideas). **There is no «Lo que me respondiste»**: what you answer to a comment, to an option they chose, to a «Hazlo tú» or to a stance is read next to its note and its step, not in «¿Dónde quedamos?», because it would tell them again what they said themselves (`pestanasDq.ts` › `lineasDeRespuestas`). **Context, only where it is needed**: a specific answer to a comment or a question says what it is about (its name between «» or its number), in a few words; no answer repeats what the person already said or confirms back what they chose.

## Validate and close

- The tests, tailored to the change (CLAUDE.md › «Tailored to the change»): in a review that touches no code, `--listo` validates the plan by itself. And that the inbox shows no errors in the route: an invalid `plan.json` shows as an error in the Studio.
- `node tools/director/bandeja.ts --listo` is the last thing that touches the plan: the Studio shows what it finds at that moment, and a change afterwards (even a sentence of the message) reaches the owner as another version of the same thing. If something must change, it is changed and `--listo` is run again before the commit.
- A local commit in the project's memory (branch `stl`), only with what is yours: `plan.json`, the archive (`director/archivo/`) if there is one, the map (`mapa.json`), `docs/` and the X-ray (the inbox gives the command; «Director (<id>): <what you reviewed>»). The team's work is saved by the Studio.
- **Minimal chat** (CLAUDE.md › «The chat carries only what is not in the Studio»): one or two lines («Revisión hecha; todo está en el Estudio») plus only what that rule lets through.
