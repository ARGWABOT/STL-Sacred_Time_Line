# The sources of the director's criterion

*Read only when the person asks where a criterion of `docs/metodo/director/deciding.md` comes from; the director does not read it in a review. Each rule of that procedure keeps its idea without the author's name; this list says who wrote it first.*

## The sources

- Project Management Institute, *A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)*, 7th edition, 2021: principles over processes, and tailoring them to each project.
- James E. Kelley and Morgan R. Walker, «Critical-Path Planning and Scheduling», 1959: the chain of dependencies decides the order (the critical path).
- Eliyahu M. Goldratt, *Critical Chain*, 1997 (Theory of Constraints): the work goes where the bottleneck is.
- Barry W. Boehm, «A Spiral Model of Software Development and Enhancement», IEEE Computer, 1988: the biggest risk is addressed first.
- Donald G. Reinertsen, *The Principles of Product Development Flow*, 2009: cost of delay and weighted shortest job first.
- Marty Cagan, *Inspired*, 2nd edition, 2017: the four product risks (value, usability, feasibility and viability).
- Jeff Patton, *User Story Mapping*, 2014, and Alistair Cockburn, *Crystal Clear*, 2004: thin slices that work end to end (story mapping, the walking skeleton).
- Bill Wake, «INVEST in Good Stories, and SMART Tasks», 2003: what a good piece of work meets.
- Jeff Bezos, letter to Amazon shareholders, 2016 (on 2015): one-way and two-way doors.
- Gary Klein, «Performing a Project Premortem», Harvard Business Review, 2007: the pre-mortem.
- Michael Nygard, «Documenting Architecture Decisions», 2011: the decision with its context and consequences (decision records).
