# STL Studio · instructions for agents

> **Asked to «initialize STL Studio» (or, in Spanish, «inicializa STL Studio»), or is `node_modules` missing in this folder?** Follow [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) before anything else.

This repository is STL Studio, the interface between you and the model, which applies the STL method. The assistant director's rules are in **CLAUDE.md**: read it whole before any task and follow it the same way with any agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another).

The essentials:
- The Studio is the only channel between the owner and the model: whatever you need from the owner is asked in the Studio, as a mission or as a decision.
- «Revisa estudio» = the review of the active project (`node tools/director/bandeja.ts`); one project at a time, with its «Dónde quedamos» summary at the end.
- Work in a project's repository is executed only with approval in the Studio, and with that project's rules.
- Who governs: how the director works (its context, the model, the skills, the permissions and the channel) is decided by the Studio, the same for every project; how the work is done inside the repository, by its rules. If both speak of the same thing, the more careful one wins; if a project rule clashes with the method, it goes to the owner as a decision (CLAUDE.md, «Who governs when the project brought its own rules»).
- If a permission is missing for what was approved, do not work around it or keep quiet: do the rest and say so in the answer and the message. What is denied goes by itself to the permissions panel («Modelo y consumo» › «Permisos»), where the owner authorizes it with a checkbox; only what that panel does not offer, or what is given outside the Studio, is asked for as a step of their mission, with «Autorizo» and «No lo autorizo» (and its full guide, if it is given outside the Studio). Never give yourself a permission without that «Autorizo». Whatever must be installed is asked for the same way (what it is, where it comes from, what it weighs or costs and what it is for), and if only the person can do it, the command goes ready in the step: installing is never a blocker.
- The owner's questions (notes with `sobre.tipo` «pregunta») are answered apart, as a report: the direct answer first, then the why (docs/metodo/director/review-cycle.md, the review cycle).
- Each project's memory lives in its own repository (branch `stl`, folder `.stl/`); the Studio keeps no project's data.
- Everything done in the Studio is project-agnostic: each function, design, optimization or critical rule serves any project handed to it, not only STL Studio, which is the pilot that tests it.
- Each person has their current mission and at least nine more behind it (ten in total, `RESERVA_MINIMA`) that they can reach without a review in between: those held for the closing (`esperaAlCierre`) do not count, nor do the next phase's while something of the current phase waits for the closing. `bandeja.ts --listo` does not close without the reserve, in any phase except maintenance and the start before the route. While the director works its queue, the person always has something to do or review: seeing or trying what was just done, the decisions each item of the director's queue needs, what only they know, and what the phase asks for when its time comes. While the director's queue has anything, it is ten, never filler: if they do not come out, the director works more of its queue in that review. Only with the route complete and the queue empty does it accept a short batch, in `plan.reservaCorta`, for that same review, with its reason, its `lote` of missions at hand and the motive `ruta`, never zero (`reservaSinRazon`, `EXCUSA_RESERVA`). The Studio shows the queue folded under the current mission («Lo que sigue»).
- Two queues, apart: each person's missions and the director's queue (what was approved or asked for and is not done, in the order of `plan.colaDirector`; `colaDelDirector`). The reserve counts only the person's missions; the director's work neither shortens nor fills it, and `--listo` does not close with something of its queue without a place in its order.
- Only a person requests a review, with a button that names it (`ORIGENES_REVISION`): nothing in the Studio requests one by itself. With «Pedir revisiones seguidas», the person also requests the next ones: as each one closes, the Studio launches another in their name while the director says its queue goes on (`plan.tramo`), and they stop when something truly needs the person, with a failure, at 80 % of the plan's limits or with «Detener». There is only the full review, with the notes, the deliveries and the queue: no button asks for less. The director may recommend one in a mission (`recomiendaRevision`, with its reason). Each computer's log (`.estudio/bitacora.jsonl`) says who requested it, with which button and when.
- The order of the missions is the strategy: each one goes where it is needed, after what it needs (`despuesDe`: the Studio holds it until that is done, and `validarPlan` does not let one go before another of the same person that it needs) and not before its time. What only serves the closing goes at the closing (`alCierre`, also the request done in the closing); decisions that change the route, as soon as possible; seeing what was done, right after it; what belongs to a future phase, in its phase, never to fill the ten. Every review checks the order of the whole queue.
- What the owner asks about the Studio does not stay in a session's context: what can be checked goes in the code, with its test and its check.
- Local commits, one per change, as soon as its tests pass; push only when the owner asks, in the Studio or with the permission in «Modelo y consumo» › «Permisos».
